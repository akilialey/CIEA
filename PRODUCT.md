## Users

- International students (Africa, North America, Europe, wider Caribbean) and their parents considering study, summer, gap year or medical school in Barbados.
- Barbadian families planning university abroad (Canada, UK, Australia, Africa).
- International institutions organising faculty-led groups to Barbados.
- Individuals or groups needing one stand-alone service (tours, transport, accommodation, speakers).

## Product Purpose

Caribbean International Education Agency (CIEA) is a Barbados-based one-stop shop for information and support in international education. Success: a visitor books a free 30-minute consultation or sends a WhatsApp message.

## Positioning

On the ground in Barbados, not an overseas consultant advising from afar. Co-creators of Barbados Study Tours. Works in both directions: brings the world to Barbados and helps Bajans go abroad. Combines higher education and Caribbean tourism experience; offers everything from a single activity booking to end-to-end placement.

## Operating Context

Visitors are often parents and students comparing options on phones, frequently via WhatsApp. Institutions evaluate logistics capability. Founder and Director: Patricia Atherley.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Pages defined by the content doc: Home, About, Study in Barbados, Programs, Services, Contact. Contact form (no backend yet). Phone, email are placeholders flagged in the doc for update. Founder story is a draft the owner will rewrite.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: teal-blue circle with a seafoam green line-drawn palm tree and waves, wordmark in spaced serif capitals. Colours from the logo must lead.
- Owner wants: modern, fresh, warm, inviting. No generic AI website look. No em dashes anywhere in copy.
- Photography: owner will supply stock photos; the build ships clearly labelled placeholders.

## Evidence on Hand

Content document only; logo available only as a phone screenshot. No testimonials, statistics, partner logos or pricing exist. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the human: a family's decision, a student's journey.
2. Show we are here: place, people, local specifics over generic tropical imagery.
3. One clear next step on every page: book a consultation or WhatsApp.
4. Honest, plain advice in plain language.
