Our Story

Why We Started CIEA

Caribbean International Education Agency was founded by professionals with extensive experience in both higher education and Caribbean tourism, two worlds that, when combined, create something unique and powerful.

Over many years working with students, families, and institutions, our founder knows the challenges: talented students from across Africa and North America and Europe who wanted to experience education in Barbados but didn't know where to begin. Barbadian families seeking to send their children to university abroad with little guidance on the process. International universities wanting to bring students to the Caribbean for unique educational experiences but lacking the on-the-ground connections to make it work.

CIEA was established to close that gap. We are co-creators of Barbados Study Tours, a program that brings students from around the world to experience the Caribbean, and that experience forms the backbone of everything we do.

What makes CIEA different is simple: We are here, not a consultant in an overseas office advising on Barbados from afar. We live here, work here, and have deep relationships with the institutions, officials, academics, and communities that make Barbados and the wider Caribbean such extraordinary places to study and grow. We offer everything from a single activity booking to full end-to-end education migration and placement services.

My goal is simple: to help students enrich their learning and achieve their goals through international education.

Patricia AtherleyFounder & Director, CIEA

Our Values

The principles that guide every consultation, every application, and every student journey we support.

  • Student-First

    Every decision we make is filtered through one question: what is genuinely best for this student?

  • Integrity

    We give honest advice, even when it's not what a family wants to hear. Your trust is our most valuable asset.

  • Global Perspective

    We think internationally while acting locally. Our Barbados roots give us an advantage no overseas consultant can replicate.

  • Family-Centred

    Parents are partners in the process. We keep families informed, involved, and at ease throughout every stage.

  • Education First

    We believe access to quality education is transformative, for individuals, families, and communities.
