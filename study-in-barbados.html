Nine reasons students choose Barbados

  • Fully English-speaking

    No language barrier. Barbados has a highly educated, English-speaking population and a culture deeply connected to the UK and the Americas.

  • Safe and politically stable

    Barbados ranks as one of the most stable democracies in the world and consistently among the safest countries in the Caribbean. Parents feel comfortable. Students feel at home.

  • Year-round sunshine

    Average temperature of 28°C year-round. Stunning beaches, a vibrant social scene, and an outdoor lifestyle that supports student wellbeing.

  • Excellent healthcare

    The Queen Elizabeth Hospital provides public healthcare, and there are several private clinics. International students have access to quality medical care.

  • More affordable than you think

    Tuition costs in Barbados are significantly lower than equivalent programs in North America or the United Kingdom, without sacrificing quality.

  • Globally connected

    Direct flights to and from London, Miami, New York, Toronto, Montreal, Lagos, Accra, and across the Caribbean. Barbados is accessible from almost anywhere.

  • Internationally accredited institutions

    The University of the West Indies, Ross University, and the American University of Barbados all carry international accreditation and globally recognized qualifications.

  • Rich culture and heritage

    A vibrant arts scene, a UNESCO-listed capital city, annual festivals, and a warm, welcoming Bajan culture that makes students feel part of a community.

  • Extraordinary lifestyle

    Glass-bottom boat tours, surfing and snorkeling, year-round nature hikes, cricket, rum distillery tours. Barbados offers an experience no campus in a cold climate can match.
