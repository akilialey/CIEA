Gap Year and Summer or Winter Abroad

We design and organize gap year and summer programmes that meet students' unique interests and needs. Whether you want to explore the Caribbean, develop a skill, volunteer, or prepare for university, we create an experience tailored to you.

What's included

  • Fully customized itinerary based on your interests
  • Dance, theatre, music, environment, sports and more
  • Accommodation arrangements in Barbados
  • Airport pickup and on-island transportation
  • Cultural and heritage tours
  • Multi-week or semester-length options

Summer Camps

We facilitate enrolment in local summer camps in collaboration with partners, in areas that students are passionate about. From the performing arts to environmental conservation, our camps combine learning with an unforgettable Caribbean experience.

What's included

  • Partner camps in dance, theatre, and music
  • Environmental and conservation programs
  • Sports camps including cricket, swimming, and more
  • Ages 8 to 18
  • Host accommodation
  • Caribbean cultural integration

Faculty-Led Education Abroad Programs

We provide comprehensive information, program design and planning support, logistical services, and in-country support for international institutions seeking to organize study abroad programmes to Barbados.

What's included

  • Program content design aligned with learning outcomes
  • Accommodation arrangements for groups
  • Airport pickup and on-ground transportation
  • Cultural, heritage, and educational tours
  • Meetings with local officials, academics and experts
  • Glass-bottom boat tours, nature hikes and activities
  • Ongoing in-country support during the program

Volunteer and Service Learning

We connect students with meaningful volunteer opportunities and service learning experiences in Barbados. Choose from education, environment, community development, healthcare, and more.

What's included

  • Volunteer placement matched to your interests
  • Education, environment, community development
  • Healthcare and social service opportunities
  • Accommodation and logistics support
  • Certificate of service completion
  • Cultural immersion alongside your placement
