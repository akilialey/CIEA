Why Choose Barbados?

A Caribbean Island Unlike Any Other

Barbados offers world-class education in one of the most beautiful, safe, and sophisticated environments on the planet.

Explore Barbados Institutions
Photo placeholderWide shot of the Barbados west coast or Carlisle Bay

  • Fully English-Speaking

    No language barrier. Barbados has a highly educated, English-speaking population with deep ties to the UK and the Americas.

  • Safe & Politically Stable

    Ranked as one of the most stable democracies in the world and consistently among the safest countries in the Caribbean.

  • Year-round Sunshine

    Average 28°C year-round. Stunning beaches, a vibrant social scene, and an outdoor lifestyle that supports student wellbeing.

  • Excellent Healthcare

    The Queen Elizabeth Hospital provides public healthcare and several private clinics ensure students have access to quality medical care.

  • Affordable Tuition

    Tuition and living costs are significantly lower than equivalent programs in North America or the UK, without sacrificing quality.

  • World-Class Institutions

    Home to The University of the West Indies, Ross University School of Medicine, and the American University of Barbados School of Medicine, all internationally accredited.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do international students need a visa to study in Barbados?

Most international students require a Student Visa to study in Barbados. Citizens of several countries may enter visa-free initially. We advise on the full visa process.

Can CIEA help me get a scholarship?

Yes. We offer dedicated scholarship search support and advice based on our institutional connections. Our team knows what opportunities exist and how to make your application competitive.

How long does the university application process take?

Application timelines vary by institution and program. Generally, plan for 3 to 6 months from initial consultation to receiving an offer letter. We recommend starting as early as possible. Contact us to map out your timeline.

Is Barbados safe for international students?

Barbados is consistently ranked as one of the safest destinations in the Caribbean. It has stable governance, low violent crime, excellent healthcare, and a culture that is welcoming toward international visitors and students.

What is included in the Barbados Study Tours support?

We provide end-to-end support: program content aligned with learning outcomes, accommodation, airport pickup, on-ground transportation, cultural and heritage tours, glass-bottom boat tours, nature hikes, activity bookings, and facilitated meetings with local officials, academics, and practitioners.

Can students work while studying in Barbados?

International students on a Student Visa in Barbados may apply for work authorization under specific conditions. We advise on legal work opportunities.

Can you help Barbadian students apply to universities in Canada or the UK?

Yes, this is one of our core services. We guide Barbadian students through the full process: choosing the right school and program, preparing a strong application, understanding visa and study permit requirements, applying for scholarships, and preparing for departure.

Do you help with accommodation in Barbados?

Absolutely. We arrange on-campus housing subject to availability and connect students with vetted off-campus options that are safe, affordable, and well-located.

What makes CIEA different from other education consultants?

We are based in Barbados. We know the island, its institutions, and its people intimately. Our advisors also have intimate, first-hand knowledge of study in Canada, the UK and Australia. We are co-creators with you of Barbados Study Tours, with a deep network across education and tourism. We offer genuine end-to-end support: not just advice, but on-the-ground logistics, in-country relationships, and ongoing student care.
